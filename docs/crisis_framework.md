# JKH Crisis Management Framework

## 1. Classification Levels
*   **Issue**: A potential problem that can be handled within normal business operations.
*   **Risk**: A situation with the potential for significant negative impact if not managed.
*   **Crisis**: A high-impact event that threatens the organization's reputation, operations, or stakeholders, requiring immediate and extraordinary response.

## 2. Eight Stages of Crisis Lifecycle
1.  **Prevention**: Measures to stop a crisis before it starts.
2.  **Detection**: Identifying early warning signs (Monitoring).
3.  **Classification**: Determining the level (Issue vs Risk vs Crisis).
4.  **Activation**: Engaging the Crisis Management Team (CMT).
5.  **Response**: Executing the communication and operational plan.
6.  **Resolution**: Bringing the crisis under control.
7.  **Recovery**: Returning to normal operations and rebuilding trust.
8.  **Post-Action Review**: Learning from the event to improve future response.

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   **Time to Detect (TTD)**: Time from event onset to platform detection.
*   **Time to Classify (TTC)**: Time from detection to severity assignment.
*   **Time to First Response (TTFR)**: Time from classification to initial action/statement.

## 4. Communication Protocols
*   **One Voice Policy**: Only designated spokespeople may speak to the media.
*   **Responsibility Language Matrix**: Using empathetic, factual, and action-oriented language.
*   **Stakeholder Prioritization**: Employees first, then customers, then shareholders and the public.

## 5. Implementation Scenarios

### Scenario A: BYD Safety Concerns & Proxy Marketing
*   **Challenge**: Managing negative public perception following accidents or safety concerns.
*   **Strategic Response**: Utilizing **Proxy Marketing** to bridge the gap between social media sentiment and reality.
*   **Action Plan**: 
    *   **Positive Sentiment Bridging**: Connect social media pages with authentic positive feedback from verified customers.
    *   **User Testimonials**: Feature real-world feedback ("I own a BYD, it works like this, I got it from [Location], and there are no issues").
    *   **Visual Proof**: Shared photos from customers showing their vehicles in use to demonstrate reliability and satisfaction.

### Scenario B: Sri Lanka Customs Import Blocking & Public Education
*   **Challenge**: Customers unable to clear vehicles or pay the intended amounts due to Sri Lanka Customs blocking/restrictions.
*   **Strategic Response**: Transparency through **Educational Content & Direct Interviews**.
*   **Action Plan**:
    *   **Podcast Strategy**: Use podcasts and long-form interviews to explain the "real issues" and technicalities behind the customs delays.
    *   **Information Spreading**: Distribute these explanations across social media, newspapers, and other news channels.
    *   **Feedback Integration**: Gather and highlight positive customer feedback once issues are explained or resolved to restore confidence in the brand's handling of the situation.
